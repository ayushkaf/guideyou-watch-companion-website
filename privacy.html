Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Guide You Watch Companion is built to keep your data on your own devices. We do not run any server that receives your heart-rate data, pairing information, or personal notes.

We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs. We do not require an account to use the app's core features. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

A note on the watch's local network connection

The watch app can connect to a companion server over your local Wi-Fi network for optional command features. This connection is not encrypted (it uses ws://, not wss://). It only operates on your own local network, is not exposed to the internet, and does not carry heart-rate data — but if you run a companion server, keep it on a network you trust.

Australian Privacy Act 1988

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, the kinds of information collected, purpose, access/correction, cross-border disclosure, security, and anonymity are addressed above; nothing is disclosed overseas except Google Play Billing's own processing. Complaints may be lodged with the OAIC.

US state privacy laws

Including CCPA/CPRA, we do not sell or share personal information. Categories collected, retention, and your rights are addressed above; because we hold no server-side copy, rights are exercised directly on your device.
