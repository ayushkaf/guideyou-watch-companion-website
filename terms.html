Not a medical device

Guide You Watch Companion is a general wellness product. It measures heart rate through a standard consumer smartwatch sensor and estimates rest periods from that trend — it does not diagnose, does not flag results as abnormal, and does not reference any specific disease or condition. Vibration, breathing, and sound features are general relaxation tools, not treatment.

Not for emergencies

If you need urgent help, contact emergency services directly — 000 in Australia, 911 in the United States.

Age requirement

This app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
